Team bots and groups

Besides your own bots, the sidebar lists:

  • Team bots: bots someone on your team shared with you (e.g. a shared assistant), marked ·team. They come from the Cursor backend's ListGrokBotTeamAgents and every call for them (send, history, delivery status) carries the viewer session that list hands out. grokbot-tui keeps that session per bot and re-binds it before each call.
  • Groups: group chats (is_group). /bots refreshes the list and shows hidden bots and groups too.

Requirements

Team bots need a Cursor token, so log in with /login desktop (recommended) or /login cursor; a plain /login gateway login can list them but can't open them. If a team bot shows in the list but its chat stays empty or sending fails, log in again with one of those (see Troubleshooting).

What works

  • Chatting, history, attachments, reactions and search work as with your own bots (search covers a team bot once its chat has been opened and synced).
  • Their settings belong to the owner: the bot tab in Settings is read-only for team bots.
  • The MCP server lists and talks to team bots too.
